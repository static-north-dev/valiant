# Code of Conduct

Valiant should be a welcoming, safe, and professional place to collaborate.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- communicate with respect and good faith;
- welcome differing viewpoints and experience levels;
- give and receive constructive technical feedback;
- respect personal boundaries, privacy, and confidential information;
- take responsibility for mistakes and make reasonable efforts to repair harm.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks,
deliberate intimidation, publishing another person's private information, and
repeated disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other spaces operated for Valiant. It also applies when someone is
officially representing the project.

## Reporting

Report possible violations privately to `it@staticnorth.dev`. Include relevant
links, context, and supporting material. Do not publish sensitive details in a
repository issue.

Reports will be reviewed as privately and fairly as practical. Depending on
severity and history, responses may include clarification, a private warning,
temporary participation limits, removal of content, or a permanent ban. The
maintainer may skip intermediate steps when necessary to protect participants.
